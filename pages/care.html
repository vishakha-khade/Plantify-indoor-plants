THE GREEN GUIDE

Plant care.

KEEP IT SIMPLE

Happy plants
need simple care.

You don't need to be a plant expert. Start with light, water and a little attention.

01

Light

Check how much natural light your plant needs before choosing its spot.

02

Water

Check the soil before watering. Too much water can be as harmful as too little.

03

Soil

Use healthy, well-draining soil so the roots have enough air and space.

04

Attention

Look at your plant regularly. Yellow leaves and dry soil can tell you a lot.
